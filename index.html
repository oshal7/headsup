    Kaise khelein?

    1. Pick a deck — Bollywood, festivals, food, brands, cricket…
    2. Put the phone on your forehead, sideways, screen facing your friends. Don't peek! 🙈
    3. Friends give clues — talk, act, or hum (each deck tells them how).
    4. Guessed it? Nod down — tilt the screen to the floor. ✅
    5. Stuck? Tilt your head back — screen to the ceiling — to pass. ⏭
    6. Most correct answers before the timer ends wins. Pass the phone and take turns!

    On iPhone, tap “Allow” when asked for motion access. If your phone has no motion sensor, the game shows tap buttons instead (handy for a friend to hold). On a laptop: the guesser turns their back to the screen; friends press Space (correct) or P (pass).
