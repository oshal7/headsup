Bujho Toh! Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy covers the Bujho Toh! Android app and the Bujho Toh! website (together, "the game").

The short version

The game does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The Android app does not even request internet access.

What stays on your device

To work, the game saves a few things only on your own device:

  • your settings (music, sound, vibration, controls, round length),
  • your best score for each deck,
  • which cards you have already seen, so they don't repeat too soon,
  • any custom decks you create.

On Android, this data may be included in your phone's own backup to your Google account if you have Android backup turned on. That backup is handled by Google under your account settings; we never receive it. You can delete all of this data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

Motion sensors and vibration

The game reads your phone's motion sensor only while a round is being set up or played, to detect when you tilt the phone. These readings are used instantly on the device and are never saved or sent anywhere. The app uses vibration to give feedback when you answer.

Sharing your score

If you tap "Share score", your phone's own share menu opens with a short message. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself.

Children

The game is suitable for families. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
